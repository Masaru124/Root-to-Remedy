# Brag Plan: Root-to-Remedy

## What is this app?
Root-to-Remedy is a permissioned blockchain (Hyperledger Fabric v2.5) and content-addressed storage (IPFS) system for Ayurvedic herb supply chain provenance—linking farm origin GPS coordinates, lab purity certificates, and retail product QR codes into a tamper-evident audit trail.

## The angle
Don't just claim your herbs are pure—prove it on-chain in under 15ms. We showcase the complete botanical journey from farm harvest to consumer QR verification, highlighting the smart contract quality gate that automatically rejects tainted batches before they can ever reach manufacturing.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A glowing neon emerald security badge pulsing against a deep dark background: "VERIFIED ON-CHAIN". Text slams in: "Is Your Ashwagandha Actually Pure?"

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **Farmer Origin & Geolocation**: Farm harvest registration with live HTML5 GPS coordinates and soil data committed to the ledger.
2. **IPFS Lab Certificate & Purity Rule**: Lab report PDF pinned to IPFS, triggering the smart contract quality gate (`Purity >= 95% AND Contamination == 0 -> APPROVED`).
3. **Approval-Gated Product QR Minting**: Manufacturer selects only lab-APPROVED batches to mint retail products and generate downloadable QR codes.
4. **Zero-Login Consumer Provenance**: Instant QR camera scan resolving to a rich provenance timeline with Google Maps location links and IPFS lab certificate viewer.

## Outro / punchline
"From Farm Harvest to Consumer Scan — Immutable Botanical Provenance." Logo resolves with GitHub repository link `Masaru124/Root-to-Remedy`.

## User flow worth showing
`Farmer Harvest (GPS Location) -> Lab PDF Upload (IPFS Purity Check) -> Manufacturer QR Minting -> Consumer Public Scan (<15ms Provenance Timeline)`.
